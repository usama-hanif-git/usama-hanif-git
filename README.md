# Hi, I am Usama Hanif 👋
MSc researcher, Department of Biosystems Engineering, Gyeongsang National University, Republic of Korea (Smart Space Sensing Lab).
I build computer vision and deep learning tools for livestock farming: detecting animal behavior from farm cameras and linking behavior to physiology, such as heat stress.

## 💫 About Me:
🔭 I'm currently working on computer vision for heat stress and behavior monitoring in cattle<br>👯 I'm looking to collaborate on precision livestock farming, animal welfare AI, and thermal + RGB sensing<br>🤝 I'm looking for help with multi-object tracking of individual cattle in farm video<br>🌱 I'm currently learning Git, GitHub, and thermal image analysis<br>💬 Ask me about YOLO models for animal behavior detection<br>⚡ Fun fact: I started in biochemistry and wet-lab work before moving to AI for animals
