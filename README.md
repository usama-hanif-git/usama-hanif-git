## Hi, I am Usama Hanif

MSc researcher, Department of Biosystems Engineering, Gyeongsang National University, Republic of Korea (Smart Space Sensing Lab).
I build computer vision and deep learning tools for livestock farming: detecting animal behavior from farm cameras and linking behavior to physiology, such as heat stress.

### About Me
- 🔭 I'm currently working on computer vision for heat stress and behavior monitoring in cattle
- 👯 I'm looking to collaborate on precision livestock farming, animal welfare AI, and thermal + RGB sensing
- 🤝 I'm looking for help with multi-object tracking of individual cattle in farm video
- 🌱 I'm currently learning Git, GitHub, and thermal image analysis
- 💬 Ask me about YOLO models for animal behavior detection
- ⚡ Fun fact: I started in biochemistry and wet-lab work before moving to AI for animals
